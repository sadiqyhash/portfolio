Smells Like Home!

In our earliest attempts at drawing, we often sketch a home, windows, a sky, and a family. The shape may vary, the faces may shift, but the concept remains: home. Yet what truly defines a home? Where is it located, and how is it remembered?

My research project explores home through the sense of smell, treating olfaction as a tool for evoking memory, tracing belonging, and reconstructing emotional geographies. Smell is directly linked to the limbic system, the part of the brain that governs memory and emotion. Unlike sight or language, scent can trigger involuntary recall, resurfacing deeply embedded feelings, images, and narratives.
