About

Sadiq Al-Harasi

Sadiq Al-Harasi is a Yemeni artist, photographer and curator based in Aarhus, Denmark, where he studies photojournalism at the Danish School of Media and Journalism (DMJX).

Artistic practice

His research-based practice examines how personal archives shape memory, identity and belonging across generations. Working with photography, moving image, sound, cyanotype, text and installation, he brings family photographs, handwritten records, domestic objects and oral histories into conversation with wider collective histories.

Through fragments, gaps and silences, his work considers displacement, absence, cultural memory and the ways personal and collective histories are preserved, fragmented and retold. Moving between documentary and experimental approaches, he treats archival materials not as fixed evidence, but as living traces through which memory can be negotiated and reimagined.
