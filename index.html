ABOUT

Sadiq Al-Harasi is a Yemeni photographer, artist and curator based in Aarhus, Denmark. His research-based practice explores memory, family archives, identity, masculinity, displacement and belonging.

Working across documentary photography, archival research, text and installation, he investigates how personal histories are remembered, transmitted and reconstructed across generations.
